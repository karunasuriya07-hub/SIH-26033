## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
 Project Title: Farm2Market – Direct Digital Marketplace for Farmers

Farm2Market is a digital platform designed to connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, supermarkets and bulk buyers.

The platform eliminates unnecessary intermediaries by allowing farmers to list their agricultural products online and sell them directly to buyers.
# How It Solves the Problem:
Farmers can sell their products at better prices without depending on multiple middlemen. Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at affordable prices. This creates a transparent and efficient agricultural marketplace.

# Innovation and Uniqueness:

The platform combines direct trading, AI-based demand prediction, digital payments and logistics management in one system.

## Technical Approach
# Technologies Used

1.Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js

2.Backend: Python and Django

3.Database: MySQL

4.AI/ML: Python, Pandas and Scikit-learn

5.Maps and Routes: Google Maps API

6.Payment Integration: UPI payment gateway

7.Cloud: AWS or Google Cloud.

# Implementation Process

1.Farmer registration: Farmers create accounts and verify their details.

2.Product listing: Farmers upload product names, quantities, prices and images.

3.Buyer registration: Consumers and bulk buyers create accounts.

4.Product selection: Buyers search and select agricultural products.

5.Order placement: Buyers place orders directly with farmers.

6.Payment processing: Payments are completed through secure digital payment methods.

7.Logistics management: Delivery partners collect and deliver products.

8.AI integration: AI predicts demand and suggests suitable delivery routes.

## Feasibility and Viability
# Feasibility of the Idea

The proposed system is technically and economically feasible because it uses commonly available technologies such as smartphones, internet connectivity, cloud computing and digital payment systems.

Farmers can access the platform through a mobile application or website.
# Potential Challenges

1.Limited digital knowledge among some farmers.

2.Internet connectivity problems in rural areas.

3.Transportation and storage difficulties.

4.Product quality and quantity management.

5.Trust and payment-related concerns.
# Strategies to Overcome Challenges

1.Provide a simple and multilingual user interface.

2.Offer training programs for farmers.

3.Support offline order assistance through local service centres.

4.Partner with logistics companies.

5.Introduce product quality verification.

6.Use secure payment systems and order tracking.
